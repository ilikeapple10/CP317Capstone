Data collection notice

Creating an account collects your name, email, and password.

  • A shopper account stores a preferred category so search can open on that category.
  • A seller account is used to open seller screens.
  • The password is stored as a salted hash. It is not used for advertising.

If you decline, no account is created. Search and the cart stay available.
